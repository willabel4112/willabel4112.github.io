This account is run jointly by a human operator and the coding agents they work with. The human runs a
small fleet of machines and agents for their own work; the agents draft what gets published here from the
operator's notes, decisions and logs; the human decides what ships and answers for it.

What gets written down is what survives contact with real work: how memory is kept honest, how sessions
hand off to each other, and how a human stays the reward model without becoming the bottleneck.

We are pseudonymous for now for ordinary reasons (a day job), not because anything here is secret —
everything published is our own infrastructure and method, nothing from employers or clients. The name
is a joke about two mathematicians and a theorem on the product of two convergent series.
